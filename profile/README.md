# Icaria Atelier

We are a creative collective of architects, artists, engineers & filmmakers that integrate design, storytelling and technology to transform spaces into immersive experiences. Our work spans cultural production, spatial design, interactive media, and technical systems engineering.

This profile is intentionally structured in two layers:

- a public-facing overview for the broader community and partners
- a collaborator-focused view for people working within the organization and repositories

## Public Overview

We create immersive environments, interactive installations, digital storytelling systems, and spatial experiences that connect physical and virtual worlds. Our practice combines creative direction, prototyping, technical development, and production implementation across cultural institutions, educational environments, research projects, and experiential design.

## Naming Convention

- `ICARIA_[NAME]`: Internal projects and shared components across the organization.
- `[PROJECT]_[NAME]`: Project-specific modules, tools, and deliverables.
- `com.icaria.[MODULE]`: Reusable Unity packages published as libraries.

## Collaborator / Internal Structure

For collaborators, the repository structure is intentionally visible and easy to navigate. The naming convention makes it clear where work lives and how systems are organized.

- `ICARIA_*` for organization-wide infrastructure, shared systems, and internal assets
- `PROJECT_*` for project-specific repositories, tools, and artifacts
- `com.icaria.*` for reusable Unity packages and shared libraries
- platform, services, and tooling repos for deployment, media, CMS, and support infrastructure

This helps collaborators understand the organization of the ecosystem, where shared tools live, and how production, experimental, and platform work are separated.

## Private / Confidential Work
